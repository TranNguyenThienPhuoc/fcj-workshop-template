Event 4

Summary Report: FCAJ Community Day - June 2026

Event Objectives

  • Share practical knowledge: Bring practical experiences, insights, and perspectives on the application of Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence (AI) from top experts in the enterprise environment.
  • Update technology trends: Provide deep insights into the future of DevOps, AI system security, and effective process automation.

Speakers

  • Steve Tran – Founder of Cloud Thinker.
  • Hieu Nghi (Renova Cloud), Kiet, and Trung (CEO Rei AI).
  • Nguyen Nguyen & Bao – Cloud Engineers at Cloud Kinetics.
  • Truong (AI Solution) & Minh Anh (Solution Sales) at Noventiq.
  • Toan Nguyen – Security Builder.

Key Highlights

The Future of Cloud & DevOps

  • Workforce shift: Analyzing the shifting demand for human resources as AI becomes deeply involved in infrastructure management.
  • Agentic Platform Architecture: An in-depth evaluation of the pros and cons between Multi-agent and Single-agent models in resource optimization and role-based access control.

Vietnamese Voice AI for Enterprises

  • Virtual Assistant Architecture: Exploring the processing flow (Speech-to-Text → LLM → Text-to-Speech) instead of the traditional Speech-to-Speech model.
  • Solving Vietnamese-specific challenges: Handling latency (real-time streaming), voice-based gender recognition, regional accents, and techniques for identifying appropriate interruption moments.

Automation with AWS DevOps Agent

  • Live Demonstration (Demo): How AI assists operations engineers by automatically collecting logs/traces.
  • Rapid Troubleshooting: Investigating the Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and proposing a Mitigation Plan, effectively reducing Mean Time To Recovery (MTTR) from hours to just minutes.

Amazon Q Applications in Human Resources (HR)

  • Recruitment Automation: Configuring AI to automatically read, scan (OCR), and match batches of candidate CVs against Job Descriptions (JD).
  • Objective Assessment: AI automatically scores individual skills, categorizes candidates, and generates visual evaluation reports for the HR department.

Internal AI System Security

  • Secure Communication: Technical guidance on configuring Private VPC and Application Load Balancers (ALB) to allow Amazon Q to communicate securely.
  • System Integration: Connecting safely with MCP Servers (internal/third-party data systems) without traversing the risky public internet.

Key Takeaways

Practical AI Architecture

  • Mastered how to deploy AI processing flows in production environments, especially meeting the stringent latency and stability requirements of specialized industries like banking and telecommunications.

AI Scaling Techniques (Integration)

  • Gained a clear understanding of using the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and Action Connectors to transform a pure chat AI into an “Agent” capable of executing tasks and securely accessing private data (e.g., Jira, OneDrive, Databases).

Identifying Process “Bottlenecks”

  • Learned how to identify time-consuming issues in enterprises (such as engineers manually searching distributed logs or HR manually reading CVs) and map them into problems that AI can thoroughly solve.

Lessons Learned & Event Experience

  • AI amplifies capability, it does not replace humans: Although AI boasts superior processing speeds, the final decision (especially in production environments or human recruitment) still requires expert approval and oversight. AI acts as a powerful Copilot/Assistant.
  • Security is the prerequisite: The core value of introducing AI into an enterprise (Enterprise AI) lies not just in intelligence, but in ensuring that internal data is not leaked or stolen. All connections must strictly adhere to Zero Trust standards.
  • Adapt to survive: The labor market is reshaping. Instead of worrying, individuals need to proactively equip themselves with the skills to work alongside AI, master prompting techniques, and build new workflows that combine technological power to increase personal value.
